Forex Trading Masterclass Course Overview

This masterclass is designed to give learners a practical understanding of forex trading. You will learn the foundations of currency markets, price action, support and resistance, technical analysis, risk management, and the psychology required to trade with discipline.

Understand forex pairs, pips, spread, leverage and sessions
Use MT5 and TradingView-style chart analysis
Apply support, resistance and candlestick confirmation
Create a risk-managed trading plan

What You Will Learn

Technical Analysis

Read market structure, trends, support, resistance, candles and price action setups.

Risk Management

Plan lot size, stop loss, take profit, risk-to-reward and account protection.

Trading Psychology

Control fear, greed, overtrading and revenge trading using a written plan.

Course Curriculum

Module 1: Forex Market Foundations
  • What forex trading is and how currency pairs move
  • Pips, spread, margin, leverage and trading sessions
  • Setting up MT5 and demo practice
Module 2: Technical Analysis
  • Market structure, higher highs and lower lows
  • Support and resistance zones
  • Candlestick confirmation and clean entries
Module 3: Risk and Trade Planning
  • Stop loss and take profit placement
  • Risk percentage and lot size planning
  • Trading journal and review system
Module 4: Psychology and Practice
  • Discipline, patience and avoiding emotional trading
  • Demo practice and setup review
  • Building your personal trading rules
